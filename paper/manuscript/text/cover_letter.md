# Cover Letter

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript **"Abundance-weighted responses and nonlinear limits to community buffering under correlated environments"** for consideration as a research paper in *Oikos*.

The manuscript asks when positive correlation among environmental exposures buffers total community abundance. Building on established covariance and population-feedback theory, it expresses the forcing prediction in abundance-weighted response slopes and tests it with a stochastic multispecies Ricker model. The central comparison preserves every slope magnitude and changes only response signs, which reverses the effect of exposure correlation. Continuous response-balance and unequal-abundance tests place the broad transition near the forcing benchmark, while exposing its nonlinear limits. At balanced responses, mean squared coefficients of variation remain 0.32 of the independent-exposure baseline despite near-complete first-order cancellation; near the rotation threshold, the benchmark predicts weak buffering where the simulation shows weak amplification. The mixed/aligned endpoint contrast persists across the examined intrinsic growth rates.

The paper addresses a priority in the response-diversity research agenda that Ross and colleagues recently published in *Oikos*: connecting environmental responses to explicit dimensions of ecological stability. It also yields a concrete empirical prediction. Exposure covariance, response slopes, and species contributions must be measured together; the presence of positive and negative responders alone is not enough.

The study uses no empirical data, field sampling, live animals, or human participants. A de-identified, uv-managed reproducibility package will accompany submission, allowing reviewers and editors to inspect the code, tests, simulation specifications, compact summaries, and figure/table workflow. The final scientific release will be maintained in a clean, versioned public repository and archived in Zenodo with a DOI.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere and follows the journal's double-anonymised review process; author-identifying information appears only in the title page and editor-facing materials.

Thank you for considering this manuscript for publication in *Oikos*.

Sincerely,

Barry W. Brook
