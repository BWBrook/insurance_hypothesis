# Figure captions (v2)

**Figure 1.** Environmental variability destabilises aligned responses more strongly than mixed responses. Simulations for $S=4$ use 1000 replicates per scenario. Panel A shows mean community variability ($\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}$) for aligned positive slopes across innovation SD $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}$ and effective exposure correlation $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$. Panel B shows mean $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ across $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ as $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}$ increases, comparing mixed response directions, aligned response directions, and the aligned-minus-mixed gap; faint bands span the range across $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$. The dotted line marks the upper edge of the core grid at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=2$, and shading marks the highest-stress region ($\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}\ge3$).

**Figure 2.** Community variability along the deterministic mixed-response assembly sequence. Scenario means of $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ are shown against species richness $S$, averaged over the shared non-negative effective-correlation values for each $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}$; faint bands span those correlation values. The sequence adds evenly spaced slopes within $[-0.5,0.5]$, so response composition and root-mean-square slope change with $S$. It describes this assembly sequence rather than an isolated causal richness effect.

**Figure 3.** Exposure correlation versus realised synchrony by slope regime at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0.5$. Simulations use 1000 replicates per scenario. Panel A shows mixed slopes ($S=2,4,8$): realised per-capita log-growth correlations fall well below effective exposure correlation as $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ increases, becoming strongly negative at low richness. Panel B shows positive slopes ($S=2,4,8$): realised log-growth correlations increase with $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and remain strongly positive. Dashed lines indicate equality ($y=x$). Panel C shows $\mathrm{corr}_{\mathrm{realised}}-\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ as richness-averaged regime means with bands spanning the richness range.

**Figure 4.** Abundance-weighted response balance determines whether exposure correlation stabilises the community. Panel A compares balanced mixed slopes with magnitude-matched aligned slopes at $S=4$ and 8. Panel B plots the high-correlation to independent-exposure ratio of mean $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}^2$ against the abundance-weighted alignment index $A$ for a fixed-norm response rotation and a constant-total-equilibrium abundance-weight path at $S=4$. Error bars are 95% Monte Carlo intervals; the dotted line is the first-order forcing benchmark and dashed lines mark $A=1/S=0.25$ and a ratio of one. Panel C shows the same nonlinear ratio for balanced and magnitude-matched aligned endpoints across $r_0$. Each scenario uses 1000 replicates, $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0.5$, and $\phi=0.4$.

**Figure 5.** Mixed-response assembly patterns across a fixed-innovation stress gradient. Mean $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ is shown against effective exposure correlation at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0.5$ for $S=2$ (panel A) and $S=4$ (panel B) across $\phi\in\{0,0.7,0.9,0.95\}$. Legend entries give $\phi$ and stationary exposure SD, $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}/\sqrt{1-\phi^2}$. Increasing $\phi$ changes temporal autocorrelation and stationary variance together; the figure does not isolate either effect.

**Figure S1.** Mixed-slope heatmap summary for community variability. Heatmaps of mean $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ are shown across effective exposure correlation ($\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$; rows) and innovation SD ($\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}$; columns) for $S=2,4,8$, using the same assembly grid as Figure 2. A single shared colour scale and one colourbar are used across panels.

**Figure S2.** Mixed-slope heatmap summary for realised community synchrony. Heatmaps of mean Loreau-de Mazancourt synchrony $\phi_{\mathrm{LdM}}$ are shown across effective exposure correlation ($\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$; rows) and innovation SD ($\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}$; columns) for $S=2,4,8$, using the same assembly grid as Figure 2. A shared colour scale and one colourbar are used across panels.

**Figure S3.** Positive-matched control (magnitude-matched aligned slopes). This 1000-replicate control compares mixed, positive-matched, and aligned-positive slope regimes at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0.5$ for $S=4$ and 8. Panel A shows mean $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}$ against effective exposure correlation; panel B shows mean pairwise realised per-capita log-growth correlation. Positive-matched slopes isolate response sign from slope magnitude.

**Figure S4.** Species-level CV diagnostic. Approximate $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{sp}}=\sqrt{S^{-1}\sum_i v_i}/(S^{-1}\sum_i m_i)$ is shown against effective exposure correlation $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0.5$, where $m_i$ and $v_i$ are the temporal mean and variance of species $i$. The grid matches Figure 3 for mixed and positive slopes at $S=4,8$.
