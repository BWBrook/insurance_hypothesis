![Fluctuation size limits the benefit of a shared environment. (A) Ratio of mean $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}^2$ under near-perfect exposure correlation to that under independent exposures, against environmental innovation SD $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}$, for the balanced mixed community (legend: balanced mixed) and the community with the same response magnitudes aligned in sign (legend: aligned, matched magnitude), both with $S=4$. Error bars are 95% Monte Carlo intervals, mostly smaller than the symbols, and no trajectory in either treatment touched the numerical floor. Arrows at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0$ mark the first-order limits of about 0 and 3.2. The dashed curve is the second-order onset for the balanced community, $1.65\,\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}^2$. Open grey circles repeat the balanced ratio from an independently seeded grid (Supporting information). The dotted line marks a ratio of one, at which correlated exposure neither buffers nor amplifies. Independent-exposure variability is indistinguishable between the two treatments, so their shared-exposure variability compares directly: the matched aligned community's mean $\mathrm{CV}_{\mathrm{tot}}^2$ was 190 times the balanced community's at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0.1$ (95% interval 187–193) and 2.4 times at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=1.3$ (2.35–2.45). (B) The same ratio against the abundance-weighted alignment index $A$ along the fixed-norm response rotation from mixed ($A=0$) to matched aligned ($A=0.8$) slopes, at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0.1$, 0.5 and 1.3. The dotted line is the first-order benchmark, $(1-\rho_{\mathrm{eff}})+\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}SA$, and dashed lines mark $A=1/S=0.25$ and a ratio of one. Open symbols mark any retained floor contact. Each scenario uses 1000 replicates, $\phi=0.4$ and $r_0=1$.](../../../analysis/reports/fig6_amplitude_boundary.png){#fig-main-2 width=95%}
