![Imposed versus realised synchrony by slope regime at $\sigma_{\mathrm{env}}=0.5$. Simulations use 1000 replicates per scenario. Panel B shows positive slopes ($S=2,4,8$): realised log-growth correlations increase with $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and remain strongly positive. Dashed lines indicate equality ($y=x$). Panel C shows the difference metric $\Delta\mathrm{corr} = \mathrm{corr}_{\mathrm{realised}} - \rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$ as richness-averaged regime means with bands spanning the richness range. Mixed slopes become strongly negative at high imposed correlation; positive slopes are positive at low imposed correlation, near zero at intermediate values, and modestly negative only at the high end.](../../../analysis/reports/fig3_imposed_vs_realised_panels.png){#fig-main-3 width=95%}
