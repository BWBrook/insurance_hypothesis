# Figure captions (draft)

1. **Figure 1 – Insurance patterns under mixed vs aligned responses**
   - Panel A: Heatmap of community CV across σ_env (columns) and ρ (rows) for mixed slopes, S=4. Cooler colors indicate lower variability; CV declines with increasing ρ and σ effects are damped by compensation.
   - Panel B: Heatmap of community CV for positive slopes, S=4. Variability increases with ρ, showing loss of insurance when responses are aligned.
   - Panel C: CV vs ρ lines at σ_env=0.5 for S=4 and S=8, contrasting mixed (solid) vs positive (dashed) slopes. Mixed lines slope downward; positive slope upward, with richness reducing amplitude.
   - Panel D: Imposed vs realised growth synchrony (mean pairwise corr). Mixed responses sit below the 1:1 line (realised < imposed), positive responses above it.

2. **Figure 2 – Richness and environmental persistence effects (mixed slopes)**
   - Panel A: Mean CV vs species richness averaged over ρ for each σ_env. CV drops steeply from S=2→4 and continues to S=8.
   - Panel B: CV vs ρ at σ_env=0.5 for φ = 0, 0.4, 0.7, 0.9, 0.95 (S=2,4). Higher φ raises CV modestly but does not reverse stabilisation.
